# Industry / Sector Page Copy — VTECH Audio Visual Solutions

Production-ready copy for the `industry` CPT. Companion to `01-homepage-copy.md` and
`02-service-pages-copy.md`.

## How industry pages render (single-industry.php)
Industry pages are **body-content driven** — there are no ACF repeater fields like the
service pages. The template renders: Hero (title + excerpt as tagline) → featured image →
**Intro (the post body — all the copy below goes here)** → "Projects in this sector"
(auto-queried from `project` posts tagged with this industry's taxonomy term) → CTA band.

Rules for every industry page:
- **Body:** paste the "Body" copy into the page editor. Headings become H2/H3.
- **Excerpt:** set it — it becomes the hero tagline.
- **Featured image:** add a real sector photo when available.
- **Projects in this sector** populate automatically — tag `project` posts with the matching
  industry term (e.g. "churches", "hotels"). No manual linking needed.
- **FAQ:** add via a **Rank Math FAQ block** — the theme only emits FAQPage schema on
  *service* pages, not industry pages, so Rank Math must own it here.
- **Rank Math schema:** WebPage is fine (no Service/FAQPage conflict on industry pages).
- **URL base:** use your industry CPT's actual base (shown below as `/industries/…` — adjust
  if your CPT registers a different base).
- **No fabrication:** no invented clients, projects, stats or prices.
- **Internal links:** link each named service to its service page with the descriptive anchor.
